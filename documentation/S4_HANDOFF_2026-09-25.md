# S4 handoff — 2026-09-25

This is a working handoff for the approved S4 plan. It does not amend
`AUDIO_SESSION_REDESIGN.md`. The saved plan is
`~/.claude/plans/snoopy-cooking-barto.md` (also pasted into the Codex task).

## Current state

- **S4a step 1 is committed** as `ab5782d6` (`fix(dice): wait for the new rate when
  changing the clock while idle`). `DiceFamilyDriver` now waits for the achieved
  rate during an idle clock apply. The commit includes late-settling simulated
  device scenarios and updated DICE and session goldens. This has host coverage,
  but the stopped-rate-change/next-start behavior still needs a Pro 24 DSP check.
- **S4a step 2 is committed** as `8723a407` on
  `refactor/session-quiet-period` and host-verified. The changes add a DICE
  400 ms restart quiet period to catalog policy; pending, re-armed restart
  requests in `SessionScheduler`; a timer callback that dispatches to the
  `AudioSessions` queue; cancellation on HAL requests, retirement, and teardown;
  timer wiring in `DriverContext`; a post-restart observer for the DICE backend;
  virtual-time session scenarios and their golden traces. Other families keep a
  zero quiet period. The review also made HAL requests cancel the pending timer
  and moved the post-restart observer to successful completion of a reconcile.
- **S4b has not started.** It is the throwaway Pro 24 DSP hardware spike for a
  wire that stays up across `StopIO`. **S4c has not started.** Its design depends
  on the spike results. No current S4a code makes streams follow device presence.
- `SleepWithTimeout`/`Wakeup` is deferred in the approved plan. The DriverKit
  SDK answers the timeout timebase question, but replacing polling also needs
  queue and bus-completion ownership work.
